The [17 October spreadsheet](../../outputs/01a14481-9568-78f3-bcf3-992c0821b972/Skart-unit-models.xlsx)

See the [shared brief](model-animation-brief.md) for style, common clips and review notes.
Entries follow units.json order. All appearances and motions are proposals for your edits.

### Nyúl (`nyul`)

- **Model:** A small cream rabbit with oversized angular ears, warm grey paws and a compact pear-shaped body. One bent ear makes the silhouette readable without accessories.
- **Animation:** Rest tucked on its haunches; travel in two compact hops with ears trailing. Its summoned arrival unfolds from a small cream puff and lands paws first; defeat folds the ears and dissolves the figure gently.
